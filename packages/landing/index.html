Sakasa turns the real surfaces of your room into gravity switches. Everything virtual falls onto the wall, the ceiling or the table you choose. Guide a glowing marble into its ring in as few flips as you can.

01 How it plays

Three gestures. No controller, no wall of text.

  1. Point

    Aim your hand at any real surface. It lights up with a grid and chevrons that show which way things would fall. On Meta VR Glasses you simply look at it.

  2. Pinch

    Pinch and that surface becomes down. Time slows for a breath, then everything lets go and lands on it: blocks, paper rolls, the marble.

  3. Hold to preview

    Keep the pinch held and a dotted path shows where the marble will travel. Let go to commit, or look away to cancel.

The first thing Sakasa asks for is the ceiling. One pinch, and the whole table of toys falls up. That is the whole tutorial.

02 Your room is the level

Puzzles are generated from your room's planes, then proved solvable before you see them.

Scene understanding gives Sakasa your floor, walls, ceiling and furniture. A solver searches every flip sequence up to four deep, so each puzzle has a known par in your room. Missing a ceiling? A labelled paper one stands in. No planes at all? A paper room appears on a virtual table.

Top-down plans of real IWER emulator rooms with puzzle 10 generated in each. Same seed, different room, different level.

04 Hands first, glasses ready

Meta Quest 3 and 3S

Hand ray plus pinch for every action. Poke-sized buttons, a palm-up wrist menu that pauses the room, and nothing that needs a controller. The real room never moves; only virtual things fall, so there is no vestibular mismatch.

Meta VR Glasses

Eye gaze picks the surface and a pinch from either hand flips it. A surface arms after a 400 ms gaze dwell so a passing glance never flips the room. The HUD moves into the centre of the narrower field of view.

06 Run it on Quest

  1. 1cd packages/app && npm install && npm run dev
  2. 2adb reverse tcp:8081 tcp:8081
  3. 3Open https://localhost:8081 in the Quest browser, accept the local certificate, press Enter your room.

Allow hand tracking and spatial data when asked. Sit down, look around for a second, then point up and pinch.
