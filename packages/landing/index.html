01 How it plays

Three gestures. No controller, no walls of text.

Point at any plane in your room to reorient gravity in real time.

  1. Point

    Aim your hand ray at any real surface to see its gravity grid.

  2. Pinch

    Pinch and that surface becomes down: virtual toys fall and stack on it.

  3. Hold to preview

    Keep pinching to trace the path, or release to flip gravity.

03 Modes & controls (Quest 3 vs Meta VR Glasses) ↓

Meta Quest 3 & 3S

Hand ray plus pinch for every action. Poke-sized buttons, a palm-up wrist menu that pauses the room, and nothing that needs a controller. The real room never moves; only virtual things fall, preventing vestibular mismatch.

Meta VR Glasses

Eye gaze selects the surface; a pinch from either hand commits. Surfaces arm after a 400 ms gaze dwell so glancing never flips the room. The HUD positions into the centre of the 74° × 68° field of view.

04 Developer quick start (Quest 3 adb reverse) ↓
  1. 1cd packages/app && npm install && npm run dev
  2. 2adb reverse tcp:8081 tcp:8081
  3. 3Open https://localhost:8081 in Quest browser, accept local cert, press Enter your room.

Allow hand tracking and spatial data when prompted. Look around for a second, then point up and pinch.
